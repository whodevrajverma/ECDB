# ECDB: Endometrial Cancer gene DataBase

**ECDB** is a manually curated, integrated central repository designed to provide a comprehensive resource of genes and proteins involved in the various stages of endometrial carcinogenesis.
Endometrial cancer is the most common cancer of the female genital tract in developed countries and is the third most common cause of gynecologic cancer death globally, following ovarian and cervical cancer.
ECDB was developed to fill the gap in specialized resources for this malignancy.

**Web Server:** https://webs.iiitd.edu.in/raghava/ecdb/index.php#

---

## About the Database

ECDB serves as an integrated platform that catalogs experimental evidence for genes deregulated in endometrial cancer.
It consolidates scattered biological and genomic information into a single, user-friendly interface to support research into the development, progression, and prognosis of the disease.

* **Data Scope:** The database currently documents evidence for **410 genes** linked to endometrial cancer.
* **Curation:** Information is manually curated from published literature, ensuring high-quality evidence for each entry.

## Key Features

### 1. Comprehensive Gene and Protein Profiles
Each record in ECDB provides a deep dive into the molecular characteristics of the gene and its product:
* **Structural Details:** Gene architecture (exon-intron structure), gene and CDS sizes, and protein sequences.
* **Functional Annotations:** Gene ontology (GO) terms, protein domains, and biological pathways.
* **Genomic Context:** Chromosomal positions, location, and specific accession numbers.

### 2. Clinical and Experimental Insights
* **Biological Phenomena:** Details on specific alterations (e.g., expression changes) that lead to endometrial cancer as evidenced by research.
* **Sample Information:** Data regarding the specific tissue samples used in the studies supporting the gene's inclusion.
* **Genetic Variation:** Information on single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) associated with the genes.

### 3. Evolutionary and Comparative Data
* **Homology:** Information regarding homology to other eukaryotic genomes, providing an evolutionary perspective on the genes involved.

### 4. Rich Cross-Referencing
ECDB provides extensive links to external web resources and other cancer databases to facilitate deeper investigation:
* **General Repositories:** Ensembl, HPRD, and OMIM.
* **Specialized Resources:** Direct links to other relevant cancer-specific databases.

## Integrated Tools and Access

ECDB is designed with a user-friendly interface to ensure convenient data retrieval for researchers:
* **Search Options:** Users can easily retrieve entries using a **Gene ID**, **PubMed ID (PMID)**, or **Target Gene Name**.
* **Data Visualization:** Integrated views of gene architecture and chromosomal mapping.

---

## Applications

* **Target Identification:** Assisting researchers in identifying key genes deregulated across different stages of endometrial carcinogenesis.
* **Biomarker Research:** Utilizing curated evidence to find potential markers for disease progression and prognosis.
* **Systems Biology:** Leveraging pathway and ontology data to understand the complex networks driving endometrial cancer.
